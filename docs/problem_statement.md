# ArguMind

## Problem Statement

Traditional AI chatbots can generate arguments on almost any topic, but they lack personalization and continuity. Every debate begins without any understanding of the user's previous debating style, logical strengths, weaknesses, or improvement over time.

As a result, users receive generic counterarguments instead of an adaptive intellectual opponent that evolves with them.

## Proposed Solution

ArguMind is an AI Debate Twin that continuously learns from every debate.

It combines:

- Long-Term Memory
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Tool Calling
- Agentic Workflows

to create an AI opponent that remembers the user, retrieves evidence from trusted sources, and adapts its debating strategy after every interaction.

Unlike traditional debate bots, ArguMind focuses on helping users improve their critical thinking through personalized debates and evidence-backed reasoning.
